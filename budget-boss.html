Hi! I'm Penny the Piggy 🐘 — your Budget Boss guide!

Imagine it's your birthday and you've just been given £20 🎁. Amazing! But now comes the really important question...

What do you do with it? Just spend it all right away? Save it? Give some away? Let me show you a secret trick that smart people use called the Spend, Save, Give rule!
1
Needs vs Wants: First, ask yourself — do I need this, or do I just want it? Food, school supplies and warm clothes are needs. A new toy or sweets are wants. Needs come first!
2
The 3 Pots rule: Split your money into three pots — one to Spend on things you enjoy, one to Save for something bigger, and one to Give to someone who needs it more.
3
Make a plan before you spend: Deciding ahead of time how to use your money means you won't run out and feel sad later. That plan is called a budget — and you're about to make one!
