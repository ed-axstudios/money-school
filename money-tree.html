🧠 Did You Know?

🐇 Compound interest is like a snowball! It rolls down a hill getting bigger and bigger. Your money earns money, which then earns even MORE money!
⏰ Time is the secret superpower. Starting at age 8 instead of age 18 can make you THREE TIMES richer by the time you're grown up!
🌰 Warren Buffett bought his first shares aged 11 — just like buying a small part of a real company. He called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world."
🔢 The Rule of 72: Divide 72 by your interest rate to find how many years to DOUBLE your money. At 6%, that's just 12 years — your money duplicates like magic!
