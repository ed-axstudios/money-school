Hey! I'm Detective Dana 🕵️ — and today we're going on a mission to catch scammers!

Imagine you're playing your favourite game online and you get a message:

"Congratulations! You've won 10,000 free gems! Click here and enter your parent's card number to claim your prize!"

Does something feel wrong about that? Your instincts are right — that's a scam! Let me teach you exactly how to spot them.
1
If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is. Free money, free prizes, free gems — scammers use exciting offers to trick you. Real companies don't give things away for nothing.
2
Never share personal or payment details. No real company will ever ask for your password, bank card number, or home address in a message or pop-up. If they do — it's a scam.
3
Urgent = danger. Scammers say things like "Act NOW!" or "Only 2 minutes left!" to panic you into doing something without thinking. Always pause. Always check with a trusted adult first.

🚩 The 6 Red Flags of a Scam

🎁
"You've won a prize!" Did you enter a competition? No? Then it's not real. Scammers say you've won something to get you excited and off-guard.
🔒
Asking for passwords or card numbers. No legitimate company ever needs your password. Ever. If they ask — close it immediately and tell an adult.
⏳
"Act NOW before it's too late!" Real offers don't disappear in 2 minutes. Urgency is a trick to stop you thinking clearly.
💌
Spelling mistakes and weird email addresses. Real companies check their spelling. A message from "Amaz0n-supp0rt@gmail.com" is NOT from Amazon.
📱
Unsolicited messages. Did you ask to be contacted? No? Then be suspicious. Scammers contact you out of nowhere on text, email, or social media.
🕵️
It feels wrong. Trust your gut! If something makes you uncomfortable or confused, stop and tell a trusted adult. Always.

🛡️ If You Think It's a Scam...

1
STOP — don't click anything, don't reply, don't share anything.
2
TELL — tell a parent, guardian or trusted adult straight away.
3
REPORT — adults can report scams to Action Fraud (actionfraud.police.uk) in the UK.
